CharGrab
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*write maps in moto format
*handle any sized brush
*handle brush larger than screen
*work without vga
*handle odd # of pixel brushes
*handle flips
*handle different chars in different palettes
*load in any # of bitplanes from 1-8
*command line switches(nuke char file,redundancy checking)
*auto-extension adding(given foo.lbm, generate foo.chr & foo.map)
*snes version

Version 1.8
*handle new pbm format IFF files(I hate EA)

Version 1.9
